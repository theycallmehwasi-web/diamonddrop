Questions

Do you need my password?

No. Only your player UID is needed. Never share your password, OTP or login details with anyone.

Where do I find my UID?

Open Free Fire, tap your profile, and the UID is the number shown under your nickname.

What if I typed the wrong UID?

Check the summary above before you pay. Diamonds go to the UID you send, so confirm it twice.
